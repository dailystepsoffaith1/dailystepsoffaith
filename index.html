The devotional

Finding Christ
in the hard days.

In the long days following her son's brain aneurysm, Brooke began recording the words, prayers, and thoughts the Lord placed on her heart.

This devotional is a testimony to God's nearness in suffering and His mercy in impossible circumstances.

Her prayer is that these reflections would become a source of hope and encouragement for others as they walk difficult roads, follow Jesus through suffering, and learn to trust Him one day at a time.

Her story

Small Steps,
Great faith.

Brooke Stubbs is a Christian wife, mother, and nurse whose ministry started through one of the hardest seasons of her life.

After her son suffered a life-threatening brain aneurysm, Brooke began putting into words the prayers, Scripture-shaped reflections, and daily mercies that carried her family through.

With the heart of a mother and the eyes of a nurse, Brooke writes honestly about fear, faith, suffering, and the steady nearness of Christ.

Her desire is to encourage others who are walking through painful and uncertain places to keep trusting the Lord, one day at a time.

Read to the end and discover the miracles we witnessed along the way.

"He is able to do more than all we could have thought or imagined."
